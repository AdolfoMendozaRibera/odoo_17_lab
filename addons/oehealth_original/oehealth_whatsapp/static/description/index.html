OeHealth Modules - Whatsapp

OeHealth's integration with Whatsapp adds the feature of instant messaging with pictures and videos. Healthcare admins can now share messages, appointment schedule,lab test results, prescription details instantly to patients via whatsapp.

Configurationn

You can create custom templates for each of specific purpose such as appointment invitation, sending lab test results, prescription, or sending some important message or files to the patient.

Send details to patient

Healthcare admins can directly whatsapp the patients whatever he requires or is needed to be sent to him directly from the patients tab using Send Whatsapp smart button.

Send Details & Files

All the necessary and crucial documents and messages can be sent by the Healthcare Admins to patients.

Appointment details, Lab test results, prescriptions & messages everything can be sent to the patients by healthcare staff.
